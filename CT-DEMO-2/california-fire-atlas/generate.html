About this visualization

The isolated point cloud is anchored to the selected NASA FIRMS hotspot detection. Nearby particles are an artistic rendering of that record, not additional fire detections. Other loaded records are not rendered in this view.

FRP uses a logarithmic color and density mapping. Retrieved weather affects only the selected hotspot's bounded artistic animation.

The 48,000-particle budget is dedicated to the selected detection. Other loaded detections remain available in the record count but are not rendered in this isolated view.

Vertical position, particle paths, and the generated pulse are visual parameters. They do not represent terrain, measured height, fire growth, or smoke transport.
